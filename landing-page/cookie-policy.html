Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how Fooda uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website or use our mobile application.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences, understand how you use the site, and improve your experience.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following categories of cookies:

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Cookies Required for the website to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation, secure areas access, and shopping cart functionality. Session
Functional Cookies Remember your preferences such as language, region, and login details to provide a personalized experience. 1 year
Analytics Cookies Help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous information about page visits and user behavior. 2 years
Marketing Cookies Used to track visitors across websites to display relevant advertisements based on your interests. 90 days

3. Specific Cookies We Use

  • fooda_session: Maintains your login session (Essential)
  • fooda_cart: Stores items in your shopping cart (Essential)
  • fooda_location: Remembers your preferred delivery location (Functional)
  • fooda_preferences: Stores your dietary and cuisine preferences (Functional)
  • _ga, _gid: Google Analytics cookies for usage statistics (Analytics)
  • _fbp: Facebook pixel for marketing insights (Marketing)

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may also use cookies from third-party services:

  • Google Analytics: For website analytics and performance monitoring
  • Facebook: For social features and advertising
  • Paystack: For secure payment processing
  • Google Maps: For location and mapping services

5. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Note that blocking essential cookies may affect website functionality.
  • Our Cookie Banner: When you first visit our website, you can choose which non-essential cookies to accept.
  • Opt-Out Links:
    • Google Analytics: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
    • Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=ads

6. Mobile App Tracking

Our mobile application may use similar technologies for tracking and analytics. You can manage these through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy > Tracking
  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads

7. Do Not Track

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature that signals to websites that you do not want to be tracked. Our platform currently does not respond to DNT signals, but you can manage your preferences through our cookie settings.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date indicates when the policy was last revised.
